Car test map

A render test with no round on it. WASD to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, click to look with the mouse (Esc lets go), wheel to zoom. Click the brick works' machines to use them as in the game: G puts down what you hold, R turns it, T throws it. F for the free view: drag to turn, WASD to fly where you look, Space up, Ctrl or C down. Drag on the left to walk, on the right to look. Use works the brick works' machines as in the game.
