      How to play

      H shows this page

      The team builds a model on the baseplate at the job site before the timer runs out. The instructions are hidden around the house, one page per step, and one of you is a saboteur. Press Done twice to hand the build in. Not sure it is right? Carry it to the quality inspector first.

      Nearly everything else is a click: open drawers and lift rugs to find pages, pin a page to the corkboard so everyone can read it, grab the dog's collar when it runs off with a page (or bribe it with a treat from the kitchen), take the broom in the basement to sweep loose bricks, fix the electrical panel when the lights go out, and ring the bell for a Brick Meeting. The saboteur can also click a hiding place to stash a page in it.
