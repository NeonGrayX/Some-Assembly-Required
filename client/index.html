      How to play

      H shows this page

      Find the instruction pages, build the model on the baseplate before the timer runs out, then press Done twice. Not sure? Carry the build to the quality inspector.

      Moving
      WASD Space Shift move, jump, sprint
      Ctrl hold walk carefully, over loose bricks without stepping on them
      V first / third person
      Bricks and builds
      Left click or E grab a brick or build, take from a bin, place held brick
      Right click pull a single brick off a build
      R rotate held brick
      G T drop (builds are set down gently) / throw
      Click the baseplate lift the whole build (to carry it to the inspector)
      I show / hide the last inspection report
      Pages
      Click a page put it in your pocket (one at a time)
      Q X B read (the page you look at, or your own) / drop / hold it up for people nearby
      Click drawers, rugs… open them: pages hide in fridges, drawers, lockers, under rugs…
      Click the corkboard pin your page there for everyone to read
      Click the dog make it drop the page it carries (sprint to catch it), or give it a treat from the jar in the kitchen
      Talking and voting
      Enter chat (only people nearby hear you, except in meetings)
      C hold talk on voice chat: people nearby hear you, everyone in meetings
      Click the bell call a Brick Meeting (one per player per round)
      1 – 5 saboteur only: swap a brick, forge your page, hide your page, trip into the build, drop bricks to step on
