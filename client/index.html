        Esc frees the mouse for these buttons. Menu, top right, brings these buttons back. Changing the build, mode or map starts a new round.

        The team builds a model on the baseplate at the job site before the timer runs out. The instructions are hidden around the house, one page per step, and one of you is a saboteur. Press Done twice to hand the build in. Not sure it is right? Carry it to the quality inspector first.

        Nearly everything else is a clicktap: open drawers and lift rugs to find pages, pin a page to the corkboard so everyone can read it, grab the dog's collar when it runs off with a page (or bribe it with a treat from the kitchen), take the vacuum in the basement to suck up loose bricks, fix the electrical panel when the lights go out, and ring the bell for a Brick Meeting. The saboteur can also clicktap a hiding place to stash a page in it.
